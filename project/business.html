About My Business

Shami's Tech and Teach is a personal business located in Kampala, Uganda. The business provides technology repair services and computer teaching support.

Services offered include:

  • Phone Repair
  • Laptop Repair
  • Software Installation
  • Virus Removal
  • Computer Lessons
  • Digital Support Services
The business focuses on affordable pricing, professional customer care, and reliable services.
